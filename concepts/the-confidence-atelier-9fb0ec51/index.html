The Atelier Approach

Dentistry is a craft. Yours deserves the slow, deliberate kind.

We built Cherry Creek Dental Spa to feel less like a clinic and more like a private studio: quieter, warmer, hospitality-minded. Every appointment is paced for conversation first, treatment second — because the best clinical outcomes begin with a patient who feels genuinely heard.

— 01

Conversation before instruments.

Every visit starts with what's bothering you, what you'd change, and what you'd never touch.

— 02

Imaging you can actually see.

Intraoral cameras and digital x-rays put your mouth on screen — so the plan is yours, not just ours.

— 03

Sedation when you want it.

From mild relaxation to deeper sedation, comfort options are offered without judgement and without pressure.
