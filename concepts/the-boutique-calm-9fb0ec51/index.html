The Experience

Not a clinic.
A quiet place to take care of yourself.

We built Cherry Creek Dental Spa for patients who'd rather feel looked after than talked at. Everything here is designed to lower your shoulders before we lean your chair — warm lighting, considered materials, hospitality-trained staff, and a team that treats dental anxiety as information, not inconvenience.

01

A room designed to settle your shoulders.

Our suite looks and feels more like a boutique hotel than a treatment room. Soft materials, wide windows, and a pace that waits for your "yes" before moving forward.

Tour the practice
02

Gentle, always.

Light-touch techniques, warm blankets, noise-canceling options, and sedation support when you'd prefer to sleep the whole thing away.

03

Education first.

Intraoral cameras and digital x-rays let you see what we see. You'll leave knowing exactly what's happening — and why we're recommending it.

04

Individualized treatment plans.

Dentistry fits into the rest of your life, not the other way around. We build a plan around your budget, timeline, and comfort — revisable at any time.

05

Insurance, gracefully handled.

We accept many plans, file claims on your behalf, help maximize your benefits, and offer payment options when the math feels tight.

Technology

Modern tools, patient hands.

The latest advances in dentistry are only as good as the way they're used. Ours are calibrated for comfort, speed, and clarity — so you understand your mouth as well as we do.

01 · Imaging

Digital X-Rays

Low-radiation, instant imaging so we can diagnose early — and keep you in the chair for less time.

02 · Clarity

Intraoral Cameras

A guided tour of your own mouth. You see what we see, on the screen beside you.

03 · Speed

Same-Day Crowns

Digitally milled and fitted in a single visit. No temporaries, no waiting weeks for a second appointment.

04 · Screening

Oral Cancer Screening

A quiet, thorough screening folded into every new-patient exam — because early matters.

Your First Visit

An hour of listening, before anything else.

Your first appointment is about orientation, not operations. We walk through your history, take gentle diagnostics, and talk — openly, unhurried — about what you're hoping for.

Most patients leave their first visit saying the same thing: "That wasn't like a dentist at all."

Book your first visit
  1. i.
    Arrival & hello

    Tea, water, and a quiet seat while we get you set up — no clipboard cliffhangers.

    ~10 min
  2. ii.
    Digital x-rays & intraoral camera

    A complete, low-radiation look at what's happening — shared on-screen as we go.

    ~15 min
  3. iii.
    Comprehensive exam

    A gentle head-to-gum exam, oral cancer screening, and a review of your comfort preferences.

    ~20 min
  4. iv.
    Diagnosis & consultation

    A plain-language walkthrough of your options, cost, timeline — and any questions you've been saving up.

    ~15 min

Insurance & Payment

The paperwork is our part.

We accept many dental insurance plans, file claims on your behalf, help you maximize your benefits, and offer payment options when a plan needs to be spread out. No surprises at checkout — we'll walk you through every line.
