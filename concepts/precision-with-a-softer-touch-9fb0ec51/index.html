Technology, translated

The diagnostics live in this room.

Our office runs on a connected suite of digital tools so you see what we see, decisions get made faster, and treatment is shaped by data — not guesswork.

T·01

CBCT 3D Imaging

Three-dimensional scans for precise implant planning, airway analysis, and root pathology in seconds.

T·02

iTero® Scanner

Goodbye gooey impressions. A wand-sized digital scan models your bite in minutes — perfect for Invisalign and crowns.

T·03

DIAGNOdent Laser

Detects decay between teeth and below the surface long before it becomes a problem you'd feel.

T·04

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

A milled, color-matched ceramic crown placed in a single appointment. No temporary, no second visit.

T·05

Digital X-Rays

Up to 90% less radiation, instant images, and easy comparison year-over-year so we can see the trends, not just the moment.

T·06

Diode Soft-Tissue Laser

Gentle, precise treatment of gum tissue with less bleeding, less swelling, and faster healing.

T·07

Intraoral Camera

A pen-sized HD camera lets us project what we see onto a screen — so every recommendation comes with a picture.

T·08

Digital Smile Preview

See your future smile on screen before veneers or aligners begin. Approve every detail in advance.

Common Questions

Asked, answered, unrushed.

If you can't find what you're looking for, we'd much rather hear from you than have you guess.

Talk to a real person. Our front desk answers the phone Monday through Friday and replies to emails within one business day.

Call (303) 835-0033 → info@cherrycreekdentalspa.com →
Modern root canals are typically painless. We use precise digital imaging, careful anesthesia, and gentle instrumentation — most patients tell us it felt like a routine filling. The discomfort people remember is the infection beforehand, not the procedure.
Our digital x-rays use up to 90% less radiation than traditional film. Most healthy adults need a full series every few years and bitewings annually, but we tailor frequency to your individual risk — never a fixed schedule.
An implant replaces a missing tooth root with a small titanium post, then a custom crown sits on top. We plan everything in 3D using our CBCT scanner, so placement is precise, healing is predictable, and the result looks and functions like a natural tooth.
It depends on what's bothering you. Invisalign moves your existing teeth into better alignment over months. Veneers reshape, brighten, or repair the front of teeth in a few visits. Many patients combine both — and we'll show you a digital preview before either begins.
Dentists understand the muscles, bones, and proportions of the lower face better than almost any other clinician. We use Botox both cosmetically and therapeutically (for TMJ and clenching) and place fillers conservatively to support the smile — not transform the face.
If you wake up with jaw soreness, headaches, or worn tooth edges, a custom nightguard can prevent thousands of dollars of damage and meaningfully reduce TMJ pain. We scan, design, and fit it in our office.
Most patients see multiple shades of improvement in a single appointment. We can also send you home with custom trays for a slower, gentler approach if you have sensitive teeth.
Tell us. Truly — even on the booking form. We offer nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV moderate sedation, and we structure visits at the pace your nervous system needs. The first visit can simply be a conversation if that helps.
