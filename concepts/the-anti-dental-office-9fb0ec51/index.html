Your first visit,
exactly as it goes.

We believe fear lives in uncertainty. So here is every step of your first appointment, in order, with how long each one actually takes. Open any section to see what happens inside it.

00:00 min You arrive. We don't hand you a clipboard. +
Paperwork is done online before you come in, so check-in is a conversation, not a form. Coffee, tea, or still water while you wait — which is usually less than five minutes.
00:10 min Digital x-rays and a 360° look with the intraoral camera. +
Low-radiation digital x-rays and a tiny camera we use to show you — on a screen — exactly what we're seeing. No guessing, no "trust me."
00:25 min Comprehensive exam and oral cancer screening. +
Your doctor reviews teeth, gums, bite, jaw joints, and soft tissues carefully. We flag nothing we're not 100% sure about. We never pressure you into same-day treatment.
00:40 min Diagnosis and consultation — in plain English. +
We walk through what's healthy, what needs attention, and what's cosmetic-only. You leave with a written plan, clear costs, and zero homework.
00:55 min Cleaning (if you're due) and what's next. +
If your gums are healthy and you're ready, we clean the same day. Otherwise, we schedule your next visit based on what's comfortable — not what's fastest for us.
