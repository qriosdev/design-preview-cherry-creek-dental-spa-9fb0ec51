01Are root canals as bad as people say?
Honestly? No. Modern root canals — done with proper anesthesia, magnification, and gentle technique — usually feel similar to having a filling placed. The relief afterwards is the part most patients notice most.
02Are digital x-rays really that much safer?
Digital x-rays use up to 90% less radiation than traditional film. We only take them when they're clinically necessary, and you can see your images on screen with us in seconds.
03What is periodontal disease, and how do I know if I have it?
Periodontal (gum) disease is a long-term inflammation that affects the structures supporting your teeth. Early signs include bleeding when you brush, tender or receding gums, and persistent bad breath. We screen for it at every cleaning.
04My jaw clicks and aches in the morning — is that TMJ?
Possibly. TMJ symptoms can include clicking, soreness, headaches, and limited opening. Often the cause is grinding or clenching at night, which a custom nightguard or therapeutic Botox can quietly resolve.
05How do dental implants actually work?
An implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Once integrated with your jawbone, it supports a custom crown that looks, feels, and functions like the original. Most patients are surprised by how routine the process is.
06Is cosmetic dentistry just veneers?
Not at all. Cosmetic dentistry can be as light as professional whitening or careful bonding — or as comprehensive as a full smile design with veneers and Invisalign. We'll always recommend the lightest touch that achieves what you want.
07Why get Botox or fillers at a dental office?
Few clinicians know your facial musculature better than your dentist. We use Botox both aesthetically and therapeutically (for TMJ), and dermal fillers to support and refine the lower face — a natural extension of smile design.
08Whitening, Invisalign, veneers — what should I do first?
Almost always: alignment, then whitening, then any veneers if needed. Straight, bright, healthy teeth often make veneers unnecessary altogether. Start with a smile design consult and we'll map the right sequence for you.
09Do I need a nightguard?
If you wake with a sore jaw, headaches, or notice flattened tooth edges, probably yes. A custom nightguard is far thinner and more comfortable than the over-the-counter version — and it protects every restoration in your mouth.
